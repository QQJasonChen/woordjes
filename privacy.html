Woordjes 隱私政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月

Woordjes（「本 App」）的設計原則是：我們不蒐集你的個人資料，也沒有自己的帳號系統或伺服器資料庫。下面如實說明本 App 會連到哪裡、資料放在哪裡。

不需要註冊

不需要帳號。買斷購買由 Apple App Store 處理，我們看不到你的付款資訊。

網路連線

單字、例句與文法解說可離線使用。以下功能需要網路：

  • 單字與例句發音：音檔從 GitHub Pages（qqjasonchen.github.io）下載播放。GitHub 可能依其政策記錄連線資訊（例如 IP 位址）。發音為 AI 合成語音，不是真人錄音。
  • YouTube 真人例句：只有在你點下播放時，才會載入 YouTube 的內嵌播放器（youtube-nocookie.com）。此時 Google 會收到你的連線資訊，並依 Google 隱私權政策處理。不點播放就不會連線。
  • 價格查詢與購買：透過 Apple App Store。

你的學習進度

複習紀錄、收藏的單字與句子等進度存在你的裝置上。若你的裝置已登入 iCloud，本 App 會把進度的壓縮備份存進你自己的 iCloud（iCloud 鍵值儲存），讓你換手機或重新安裝後能還原。這份備份由 Apple 保管，我們無法讀取。刪除 App 不一定會清除 iCloud 上的備份。

跟讀（麥克風與語音辨識）

只有在你按下跟讀的麥克風按鈕時才會啟用。裝置支援時，辨識在手機上完成；不支援時，會使用 Apple 的語音辨識服務。錄音不會存檔，也不會傳給我們。

提醒通知

每日提醒是在你的裝置上產生的本機通知，不經過任何伺服器。

沒有廣告、沒有第三方分析

本 App 不含廣告，也不含第三方分析、當機回報或追蹤工具。

兒童隱私

我們不蒐集任何人（包含兒童）的個人資料。YouTube 內容由 Google 提供，其處理方式見上方說明。

聯絡我們

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: October 2026

Woordjes does not collect your personal data, and has no account system or backend database of its own. Here is exactly what the app connects to and where your data lives.

  • No sign-up. There are no accounts. The one-time purchase is handled by the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details.
  • Network use. Words, example sentences and grammar notes work offline. These features need a connection: (1) word and example audio, streamed from GitHub Pages (qqjasonchen.github.io), which may log connection data such as your IP address. The audio is AI-generated speech, not human recordings. (2) YouTube example clips, loaded only when you tap play, through YouTube's embedded player (youtube-nocookie.com); Google then receives your connection data under Google's Privacy Policy. (3) Price lookup and purchases, through the App Store.
  • Your progress. Study progress is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, a compact backup is also kept in your own iCloud (iCloud key-value storage) so you can restore it on a new device. Apple holds that backup and we cannot read it. Deleting the app may not remove the iCloud backup.
  • Shadowing (microphone and speech recognition). Used only after you tap the microphone button. On supported devices recognition runs on the device; otherwise Apple's speech recognition service is used. Recordings are not saved and are not sent to us.
  • Reminders. Daily reminders are local notifications created on your device.
  • No ads or third-party analytics. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • Children. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children. YouTube content is provided by Google.
